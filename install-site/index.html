FAQ

What is IronDivi?

IronDivi is a modern Rust implementation of the Divi full node. This wallet extension lets you manage DIVI on Divi mainnet and testnet directly in your browser, and switch between them at any time.

Is my recovery phrase safe?

Yes. Your recovery phrase is encrypted with your password using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption. It's stored locally in your browser and never sent to any server.

Can I use the same wallet on multiple browsers?

Yes! Simply import your recovery phrase into the extension on each browser where you want to use it.

What happens if I forget my password?

You can always restore your wallet using your recovery phrase. Make sure to keep your recovery phrase backed up securely!
